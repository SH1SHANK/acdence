### Multi-Course Diploma OPPE Allocation Policy

For students taking multiple courses with OPPEs:

1. **2 Courses with OPPEs**: Both courses will be scheduled on **Sunday, December 20, 2026**.
2. **3 Courses with OPPEs**: OPPEs for 2 courses on **Sunday, December 20, 2026**, and 1 course on **Sunday, January 3, 2027**.
3. **4 Courses with OPPEs**: OPPEs for 2 courses on **Sunday, December 20, 2026**, and 2 courses on **Sunday, January 3, 2027**.
4. **Rescheduling Constraints**: If a student fails subjects on Dec 20, 2026 and is rescheduled for Jan 3, 2027, **there is no option to split or reschedule further**. All rescheduled exams must be written on the same day.
5. **Clash Policy**: Only students with non-movable conflicting engagements on Sunday, Dec 20, 2026 who submit verified documentary proof in advance will be accommodated.

## 3. OPPE Eligibility, SCT, & Repeat OPPE Rules

### OPPE System Compatibility Test (SCT)

- Every student taking an OPPE must successfully complete the **OPPE System Compatibility Test (SCT)** beforehand.
- **OPPE 1 and OPPE 2 will NOT be scheduled for students who fail to complete the OPPE SCT exam.**
- SCT verifies webcam, microphone, screen share, browser lockdown, and network bandwidth.

### Repeat OPPE Criteria (w.e.f. Jan 2026 Term, updated Jan 05, 2026)

1. **Prior Term OPPE-Eligible Students**: Students who were OPPE eligible and received an `I_OP` in the May 2026 term, and have registered as Repeat OPPE for the September 2026 term, **continue to be considered eligible** for OPPE in September 2026 without re-qualifying.
2. **Prior Term OPPE-Ineligible Students**: Students who were marked `OPPE Ineligible` with `I_OP` or `I_BOTH` and registered as Repeat OPPE or Repeat OPPE & End Term **must regain OPPE eligibility in the September 2026 term** by submitting the required weekly assignments and completing the OPPE SCT.
3. **Failure to Regain Eligibility**: If OPPE eligibility is not attained by the cutoff date, OPPE will not be scheduled and a **`U` grade** will be awarded for the September 2026 term.

## 4. Bonus Marks & Discourse Forum Badges

### Bonus Marks Policy

Bonus marks incentivize engagement and additional academic activities.

- **Pass Gate**: Bonus marks are added **ONLY for students who pass the course** ($T \ge 40$). They impact the letter grade ($S\dots E$) but **cannot be used to cross the passing threshold**.
- **Mock Tests Bonus (0–2 marks)**: If the average of marks obtained in all mock tests conducted before Quiz 1 and Quiz 2 is $\ge 40/100$ and completed within the specified time, the student receives **2 bonus marks**.
- **Additional Course Activities (up to 3 marks)**: Course teams may set up additional activities eligible for up to 3 bonus marks. If no additional activities are configured, this component is omitted. Total course bonus is capped at 5 marks.

### Discourse Forum Badges

Active engagement in course Discourse forums is recognized via term badges:

- **Requirement**: Minimum reading time of 1 hour per week per course reading posts.
- **Badge 1**: Read time of up to 4 hours per course in a term.
- **Badge 2**: Read time of up to 8 hours per course in a term.
- **Badge 3**: Read time of 12 hours or more per course in a term.
  _(Applicable once official BS student roll numbers are active)._

## 5. Academic Integrity & Malpractice Policy

1. **AI / LLM Prohibition**: In each programming assignment, whether regular GrPA, VM test, or remote OPPE, using LLMs (e.g. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) partially or completely is strictly considered plagiarism unless explicitly authorized.
2. **Honor Code Violation**: Unauthorized AI usage constitutes an Academic Honor Code violation resulting in disciplinary committee hearings and grade penalties.
3. **Plagiarism & Code Sharing**: Do not share solutions prior to deadlines. When code similarity is detected by automated checkers (MOSS / AST diff), both parties are penalized equally regardless of authorship.

- **OPPE 1**: November 22, 2026 (Remote Online Proctored, **Syllabus Weeks 2–6**)
- **OPPE 2**: December 20, 2026 & January 3, 2027 (Remote Online Proctored, **Syllabus Weeks 2–9**)

#### Weekly Graded Programming Assignments (GrPA)

- $A_2\dots A_8$: Weekly graded programming assignment scores in Weeks 2 to 8.
- $\text{GAA} = \text{Average of best 6 out of the 7 weekly GrPAs } (A_2\dots A_8)$.

#### Eligibility Criteria

1. **OPPE 1 Eligibility**: Complete OPPE SCT AND $A_2 \ge 40 \land A_3 \ge 40 \land A_4 \ge 40$.
2. **OPPE 2 Eligibility**: Complete OPPE SCT AND $A_5 \ge 40 \land A_6 \ge 40 \land A_7 \ge 40 \land A_8 \ge 40$ AND Best 5 of first 7 weekly assessments $\ge 40/100$.
3. **End Term Eligibility**: Best 5 of first 7 weekly assessments $\ge 40/100$ AND attended $\ge 1$ in-centre quiz ($Q_{z1}$ or $Q_{z2}$).
4. **Course Grade Eligibility**: Attend End Term exam AND score $\ge 30/100$ in at least one programming exam ($PE_1$ or $PE_2$).

#### Final Course Score ($T$)

$$T = 0.05\,\text{GAA} + 0.20\max(PE_1, PE_2) + 0.45F + \max\bigl(0.20\max(Q_{z1}, Q_{z2}),\; 0.10Q_{z1} + 0.20Q_{z2}\bigr) + 0.10\min(PE_1, PE_2)$$
_(Where $0.10\min(PE_1, PE_2)$ represents the built-in OPPE bonus)._

_Diploma in Programming | 3 Credits_

- **Quiz 2**: **No Quiz 2 for this course**

- **OPPE**: December 20, 2026 (Remote Online Proctored, **Syllabus Weeks 1–9**)
- **Re-OPPE**: January 3, 2027 (Remote Online Proctored)

#### Biweekly Programming Tests (BPTs)

- Conducted in the course VM, 4 questions per test, 7-day submission window:
  - **BPT 1**: Oct 16, 2026 – Oct 23, 2026
  - **BPT 2**: Oct 30, 2026 – Nov 06, 2026
  - **BPT 3**: Nov 13, 2026 – Nov 20, 2026
  - **BPT 4**: Dec 04, 2026 – Dec 11, 2026
- $\text{BPTA} = \text{Average of all 4 BPT scores}$.

#### Eligibility Criteria

1. **OPPE Eligibility**: Average of first 3 BPTs $(\text{BPT}_1, \text{BPT}_2, \text{BPT}_3) \ge 40/100$. _(Note: $\text{BPT}_4$ is strictly excluded)._
2. **Re-OPPE Eligibility**:
   - Mandatory attendance on Dec 20, 2026 OPPE.
   - Minimum score of $20/100$ in Dec 20 OPPE.
   - Active presence in Google Meet proctoring session for at least 1 hour 30 minutes (90 mins).
   - **Crucial Rule**: _If absent on Dec 20, you DO NOT get another chance on Jan 3._
3. **End Term Eligibility**: Best 5 of first 7 weekly assessments $\ge 40/100$ AND OPPE must be eligible.
4. **Course Grade Eligibility**: Attend End Term exam AND programming exam score (OPPE / Re-OPPE) $\ge 40/100$.

#### Final Course Score ($T$)

$$\text{GAA} = \text{Average of best 9 out of 10 weekly graded assignments}$$
$$T = 0.05\,\text{GAA} + 0.25Q_{z1} + 0.30\,\text{OPPE} + 0.30F + 0.10\,\text{BPTA}$$

## 7. Grade Boundaries & Incomplete Status Summary

### Standard Grade Scale

| Grade |  T Score Range  | Status                                          |
| :---: | :-------------: | :---------------------------------------------- |
| **S** |   $T \ge 90$    | Pass — Outstanding                              |
| **A** | $80 \le T < 90$ | Pass — Excellent                                |
| **B** | $70 \le T < 80$ | Pass — Very Good                                |
| **C** | $60 \le T < 70$ | Pass — Good                                     |
| **D** | $50 \le T < 60$ | Pass — Satisfactory                             |
| **E** | $40 \le T < 50$ | Pass — Low Pass                                 |
| **U** |    $T < 40$     | Unsuccessful / Fail (Must repeat entire course) |

### Incomplete Status Codes

|     Code     | Trigger Condition                                                                                           | Status Meaning & Registration Rights                                                                                                      |
| :----------: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|   **`I`**    | Absent for End Term alone; all other components completed.                                                  | Register for **End Term alone** in subsequent term. Quizzes and OPPEs will NOT be rescheduled; previous coursework marks carried forward. |
